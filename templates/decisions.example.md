# Settled decisions

One bullet per decision the team has made and does not want reviewed again. Give the
reason after a colon; the critic reads this list and withholds findings that would only
reopen an item on it. Keep it short and honest: a decision here is closed for every run.

- Example: the footer contact address is the real one and stays: it is the business's address.
- Example: lists stay flat with dividers, not cards: the brand system is content-first.
