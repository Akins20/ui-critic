# <Product name> storefront brief

## Product
What it is, in two sentences, and the one thing a first-time visitor must understand.

## Audience
Who they are, what devices and networks they use, what they compare you with, what they are wary of.

## Brand system (decided; work within it)
Colours, type, theme (light or dark first), the visual stance (flat, editorial, playful), and anything that must not change.

## Ignore (not UI)
Placeholder images, seed data, admin-managed copy, dev tool badges, known work in progress.

## What matters most
1. The first question a visitor needs answered within one screen.
2. The hierarchy on the money-making page.
3. Mobile ergonomics.
4. Trust signals.
5. Consistency across pages.
6. Accessibility basics visible in screenshots.

## Benchmarks
Two or three products the visitor already knows, and what each does well that you want to match.
